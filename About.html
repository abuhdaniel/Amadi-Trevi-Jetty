About Amadi Trevi Jetty

Delivering Safe and Reliable construction and Marine Operations

Company Overview

Amadi Trevi Jetty is a marine and construction operations yard located in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. We provide heavy lifting, concrete batching, marine logistics, equipment maintenance, and material handling services to support industrial and offshore projects.

Our Mission

To provide safe, efficient, and high-quality marine and construction support services while maintaining international operational standards.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading jetty service providers in Nigeria’s marine and construction sector.

Safety Commitment

Safety is our highest priority. Our Safety Officers ensure compliance with site regulations, conduct daily inspections, and promote a zero-incident work culture.

Core Services

  • Marine Logistics Support
  • Concrete Batching Production
  • Heavy Equipment Lifting Operations
  • Industrial Equipment Maintenance
  • Material Handling & Yard Management
